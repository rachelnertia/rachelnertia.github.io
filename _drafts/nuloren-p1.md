This is some homebrew Warhammer: Age of Sigmar lore I've been meaning to write down in one place for a long time. I first came up with the idea for the Free City of Nuloren way back in 2019, or perhaps earlier, around when the first Cities of Sigmar battletome came out. Lots of people have come up with their own Warhammer army subfactions, locations and characters over the decades, and Nuloren is just another drop in that ocean of imagination. In 2020 through to 2022 I GM'd a Soulbound campaign set in and around the city, which fleshed out the sub-setting significantly. In spite of the years that have passed since that campaign, and the gradual turn of the Cities of Sigmar faction away from the cosmopolitan(/neapolitan?) vision Nuloren exemplifies,  the location and its inhabitants still occupy a large place in my brain and, more importantly, my heart.

## The Vardenfold

The Free City of Nuloren lies in a region of Ghyran known as the Vardenfold. It is much like any other healthy piece of the realm: green, dense and vicious in its vitality. It is a long voyage by air or sea from the Everspring Swathe, with no known realmgates connecting it directly to that area.
