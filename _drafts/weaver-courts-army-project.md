Since September 2025 I've been building, painting and reviewing Weaver Courts kits for Tabletop Battles (formerly Goonhammer). The Weaver Courts are the latest faction to be added to [Para Bellum](https://www.para-bellum.com/)'s *Conquest*. They're an original and long-anticipated take on the sylvan-elf-and-fairies archetype every fantasy setting should represent, well-realised in artwork and [lore](https://lore.para-bellum.com/weaver-courts) and mostly well-realised in its model range. ### Painting

The Weavers are divided into Seasonal Courts, of the seasons you'd expect: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter. Certain units belong to a Court while others are unaligned and take on a season at list-building time or during the battle (depending on which version of *Conquest* you're playing). Consequently a Neapolitan approach to painting the army makes more sense than picking one scheme and applying it to everything. I think as long as some common elements (e.g. colours, shadows, basing elements) bind units together, it's quite possible to have an army of any faction look cohesive even if a lot of different uniforms are represented[^1], or even if there is no uniform at all.

[^1]: Classic Empire armies consisting of troops from multiple states come to mind.

The first model I painted, **Oberon: Consort of Summer**, I went with a two-tone scheme: yellow-green carapace and pink flames. This then got mirrored across a lot of the army. In a sense I kind of messed up by painting my most Court of Summer-y dude in what I now think of as more Springy colours, but I've come to the conclusion that distinguishing between Spring and Summer is a mug's game. I just don't know how to do it reliably[^2].

I was given 24 of the **Kern Skirmishers**, the main infantry unit, who are not tied to any particular Court. I didn't feel like doing them all in the same colours, so I painted 12 in a Spring/Summer theme matching Oberon and 12 in an Autumn theme.

`TODO: Kern Skirmishers photo`

I find these models a little hit and miss. Three out of the four possible back-attachments look cool, but the flappy wing/leaf ones don't really look like anything to me and also don't look like they fit with the rest of the design. It's possible to get some cool poses, but also some goofy ones, and I think they look really dumb when ranked up for *The Last Argument of Kings*[^3].

[^3]: Models designed for rank and flank games should not be very dynamic in my opinion. They should be designed to look good in formation first and foremost, not to look good as individuals.
