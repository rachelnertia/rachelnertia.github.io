Since September 2025 I've been building, painting and reviewing Weaver Courts kits for Tabletop Battles (formerly Goonhammer). The Weaver Courts are the latest faction to be added to [Para Bellum](https://www.para-bellum.com/)'s *Conquest*. They're an original and long-anticipated take on the sylvan-elf-and-fairies archetype every fantasy setting should represent, well-realised in artwork and [lore](https://lore.para-bellum.com/weaver-courts) and mostly well-realised in its model range. You can see my takes on the varioud model kits I've reviewed over on Tabletop Battles:

- [Oberon](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-oberon-model-review/)
- [Kern Skirmishers](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-kern-skirmishers-model-review/)
- [Cran Guardians](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-crann-guardians-model-review/)
- [Mhor Sidhe and Seanchas Sidhe](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-mhor-sidhe-and-seanchas-sidhe-model-review/)
- [Coill Draic](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-coill-draic-model-review/)
- [Scathach Hunters](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-scathach-hunters-model-review/)
- [Gemred Knights and Scaile Dancers](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-gemred-knights-and-scaile-dancers-model-review/)
- [Ciannir Riders and Will O' Wisps](https://www.goonhammer.com/conquest-weaver-courts-ciannir-riders-and-will-o-wisps-model-review/)

I'm not reviewing any more for the foreseeable future as I settle into new actual work and I want to focus on hobby projects that don't have strings attached. As great as getting free models, and being paid to write about them, has been, it's just not as much fun as following my butterfly.
