Just painted up Zikkit's Tunnelpack from [Warhammer Underworlds: Embergard](https://www.warhammer.com/en-GB/shop/warhammer-underworlds-embergard-2024-eng). They're the first Skaven I've painted in about 7 years, the last being the original Skaven Underworlds warband, Spiteclaw's Swarm. Since then I've evolved a lot as a painter and gained access to new materials, like Contrast paints. 

<!--more-->

I'm starting in on my stash of other Skaven models, beginning with the Clan Eshin models from [Spearhead: City of Ash](https://www.warhammer.com/shop/spearhead-city-of-ash-2026-eng) and then moving, at long last, on to the contents of Skaventide, so I used this warband as an opportunity to dial in on how I want my Skaven army to look overall.

![](/images/2026/05/colour-scheme.png)

I'm going for a split-complementary with pink as the primary colour, used in various hues and values on clothes and skin. Then the complementary colours are green, erring yellow, for warpstone, gadgets and eyes; and cyan/turquoise/teal, represented as verdigris on armour and metal devices.

![](/images/2026/05/zikkit.jpg)

I began with a black-to-light-grey zenithal prime and a white all-over drybrush.

Steel: 

- Vallejo Metal Color (henceforth VMeC) Steel
- Gryph-Hound Orange and/or Skrag Brown watered down selective wash for rust
- VMeC Silver highlights

Bronze:

- VMeC Copper
- Aethermatic Blue wash
- Nihilakh Oxide for extra oxidation
- VMeC Silver edge highlights

Wood:

- Wyldwood
- Zandri Dust + Ushabti Bone

Warpstone, Fire and Tech:

- Warp Lightning
- Moot Green highlights
- Flash Gitz Yellow peak highlights

Skin and Tail:

The grenade-chucking Skaven uses a different recipe but I won't be carrying it forward as it turned out too dark and muddy.

- Carroburg Crimson
- Volupus Pink nose, mouth

Fur:

- Vallejo Model Color (henceforth VMoC) Dark Grey
- VMoC Neutral Grey
- Druchii Violet wash
- VMoC Light Grey highlights

![](/images/2026/05/zikkit-back.jpg)

Leather:

- Snakebite Leather or Black Templar
- Zandri Dust/Ushabti Bone highlights
- Druchii Violet wash

Cloth: 

- Sigvald Burgundy
- Ushabti Bone highlights, sometimes glazed back with Sigvald Burgundy or Magos Purple or Volupus Pink

![](/images/2026/05/wheel.jpg)
*Colour wheel courtesy of [HTML Color Codes](https://htmlcolorcodes.com/color-wheel/)*

I'll be going for ashy grey bases on the rest of my skaven, but the Tunnelpack's sculpted bases represent the mines beneath Embergard, in Aqshy, so I painted them with ruddy earth and sinister red Emberstone-infused puddles.

- Bugman's Glow earth
- Dryad Bark wood
- Zandri Dust drybrush
- VMeC Burnt Iron metal
- Khorne Red + Mephiston Red + Evil Sunz Scarlet puddles
- Agrax Earthshade/Reikland Fleshshade wash
- Zandri Dust + Ushabti Bone highlights on stones, wood
- VMeC Silver edge highlights on metal

I have an itch to play some more Underworlds, so hopefully these guys will see the table soon!
