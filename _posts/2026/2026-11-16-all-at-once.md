A lot is going on. To recap:

1. My fellow dismissed are about to enter the final days (Tuesday 17th and Wednesday 18th November) of our legal battle against Rockstar Games.
2. After months of negotiation, a ballot has begun at Rockstar Games over whether the company should formally recognise IWGB Game Workers, granting union members there the power to collectively bargain with management.
3. GTA VI comes out on November 19th.
4. My 33rd birthday also comes out on November 19th[^1].
5. I had surgery less than two weeks ago.

😓

Of the above I'm most anxious about 1 and 2, both of which I'm (unsurprisingly) personally and professionally invested in, but over which I have no control and little influence.  The former is in the hands of our legal team (who have done an absolutely amazing job so far) and the employment judge. The latter is in the hands of the employees at Rockstar, who are free to exercise the democratic power they've been granted (*thanks in no small part to myself*, I think proudly) whichever way they see fit. If I was still there I'd be doing my damnedest to persuade people to vote for recognition, to dispel the myths, to [fight the fear](https://bsky.app/profile/austinkelmore.com/post/3mv5o2dlxx22h)... come to think of it, perhaps that's why I'm no longer there?

I've resigned myself to not feeling great about 3. A mounting dread about it has been building to a head as I scroll through my former colleagues celebrating their achievement on social media, walk under billboards and past bus stop ads, and hear the volume of discourse about the game in the media and everywhere (including my new job) ramp up to 11. At the airport check-in desk on Friday the clerk saw my backpack, still adorned with its Rockstar badge, and asked if I worked there. "Used to," I said. "Did you work on GTA 6?" he followed up. "Used to," I said again. I have good reason to believe this feeling will only get worse over the coming year. It might be a long time before I'm free of it.

Will 3 overshadow 4? No, I don't think so. My two wonderful partners (who helped a *lot* with 5) will be taking me out and keeping me entertained. It's good to love, be in love, be loved, etc. 

Regarding 5, I now need to figure out the right way to balance my eagerness to get back to work with the need to recover. That's never easy!

[^1]: Yes, they fired me and then, a week later, delayed the game to my birthday. I'm sure it's just a coincidence, but still: ouch!
